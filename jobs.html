Current Vacancies

Xì Mi Village Studio is a creative agency that designs and builds websites, brand identities and digital content for clients across Vietnam. We are growing our web team and are looking for people who care about clean code, thoughtful design and accessibility.

Xì Mi Village Studio welcomes applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. We are committed to building a workplace that respects and values First Nations perspectives.

How to Apply

Read each role carefully, then complete the online application form.

  1. Note the reference number for the role.
  2. Check that you meet the essential requirements.
  3. Complete the application form.

Front-End Web Developer

Reference: FE001

Build responsive, accessible websites for our clients using HTML5, CSS3 and modern front-end practices. You will work closely with designers to turn concepts into polished, validated pages.

Salary: $85,000 to $100,000 plus superannuation. Reports to: Head of Development.

Key Responsibilities

  • Convert design mockups into semantic, standards-compliant HTML and CSS.
  • Ensure client sites follow applicable WCAG accessibility guidance.
  • Maintain shared component libraries and style guides.
  • Collaborate with designers and back-end developers in agile sprints.

Requirements

Essential

  1. Strong knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3, including Flexbox and Grid.
  2. Experience making websites accessible and validating code.
  3. Working knowledge of version control with Git and GitHub.

Preferable

  • Experience with a JavaScript framework such as React or Vue.
  • Familiarity with Jira or other agile project tools.

UI/UX Designer

Reference: UX002

Shape the look, feel and usability of client websites and digital products. You will research user needs, create wireframes and prototypes, and deliver design systems that developers can build from.

Salary: $80,000 to $95,000 plus superannuation. Reports to: Creative Director.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity layouts for client projects.
  • Run user research and usability testing to guide design decisions.
  • Build and maintain a consistent design system with colour and type standards.
  • Present design concepts to clients and respond to feedback.

Requirements

Essential

  1. A portfolio showing web or app design work.
  2. Proficiency in design tools such as Figma or Adobe XD.
  3. An understanding of accessible colour contrast and layout.

Preferable

  • Basic HTML and CSS knowledge to communicate effectively with developers.
  • Experience designing for government or education clients.
